# Supplied young referee training guide

The user supplied **PDSA Young Referee Training Guide** on 2026-10-28. The original
text is preserved in [askref-young-referee-training-guide.txt](askref-young-referee-training-guide.txt).
Its 75 numbered questions are stored in `src/lib/askref-training-questions.json`.

The guide supplies question coverage, short explanation ideas and calm sideline
communication advice. It is not treated as an official rule document. It says
the PDSA PDF could not be retrieved and references IFAB 2026/27. AskRef remains
pinned to the repository's PDSA 2025/2026 Issue 09 summaries and IFAB 2025/26.

`src/lib/askref-training.ts` rewrites the answers against those sources. The
75 additional review-bank entries remain drafts. They stay off the user screen;
supported direct lookups and conversational division clarifications are available
without live inference. Concrete incident descriptions with extra facts are not
matched to a nearby canned ruling. Communication suggestions are conditional on
the referee's actual observations; no match-clock value is invented.

## Differences and gaps retained explicitly

- PDSA's small-sided indirect-kick/no-card rules, U6 kick-ins, U8 teaching retakes
  and division-specific offside rules take precedence over general guide examples.
- The lightning alarm is established in the bundled weather summary. The guide's
  10-mile distance and 30-minute waiting period are not. Those questions direct
  the referee to field officials and current league procedure before resuming.
- Later original-PDF checks confirmed goalkeeper use, kick-in and substitution procedures, play-down conditions, and the injury-clock instruction. Build-out lines, punting restrictions and some special-case procedures still require confirmation. See [expanded guide notes](ASKREF_EXPANDED_TRAINING.md).
- A goalkeeper holding beyond eight seconds gives a corner under the bundled
  PDSA rule. This is distinct from prohibited pass-back handling.
- A defender's deflection/save does not automatically reset offside. Returning
  to one's own half does not erase a previous offside position and later offence.
- Advice about respectful communication is teaching guidance, not a newly
  invented spectator sanction or a claim to have verified the PDSA parents guide.
