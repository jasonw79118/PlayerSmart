# Tournament and U6 operations

Admin **Schedule** opens on **Matches**, with division tabs and a compact summary. Use **Edit scheduling settings** for dates, kickoff times, fields, rest limits and day priorities; close the editor to return to the match list. Filter matches by competition or status. **Manage** opens the selected match's referee assignment, change reason, cancellation/postponement and replacement suggestions. Whole-day postponement is collapsed below the list. **Holidays & breaks** and **Schedule repair** are separate sections; switching sections preserves unsaved form entries and pending previews.

Admin **Schedule → Holidays and school breaks** saves named inclusive date ranges, applying to all divisions by default or only selected divisions. **Include the weekend following the break** also excludes Saturday and Sunday at the end of that week. A range already ending Sunday does not exclude another weekend. If a break ends earlier in the week, intervening weekdays remain available. Enter Good Friday through Easter Sunday to close the entire Easter weekend. Dates are entered explicitly for the relevant season and school calendar.

These exclusions apply to regular-season generation, tournament rounds, individual makeup suggestions, and schedule repairs, regardless of weekday priorities. Existing affected fixtures are listed for review. **Postpone affected unplayed games**, then **Preview schedule repair** and apply the reviewed replacement times. Started/finalized games stay protected. Removing a no-game period reopens its dates without silently restoring postponed games.

Admin **Tournaments** contains separate division settings. Every selected division head coach is a participant, regardless of standing. Finalized regular-season cards determine seeds using PDSA Appendix C: 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, then goal difference, two-team head-to-head and goals scored. Finish or cancel outstanding unplayed regular-season matches before publishing. Confirm remaining seed ties explicitly; alphabetical order is only a provisional display order. Seeds and participants freeze when the opening round is published.

Tournament dates, loss limits, bye seeds and extra reserved minutes are saved per division as they are edited, so Admin can prepare them before seeding opens. Choose one, two or three losses before elimination. Enter the seed numbers receiving opening byes (for example, `1,2,3`). The remaining teams must be even in number, with at least two teams playing. All teams remain eligible until they reach the loss limit. This is a **round-based loss-limit tournament**, not a fixed winners/losers bracket. Each round pairs remaining teams after prior results are finalized and avoids rematches where possible. Later odd-team byes prioritize teams with the fewest prior byes, then seed. Byes never count as wins or losses. The last remaining team is the champion.

Set tournament dates and extra reserved minutes for overtime/shootouts. PDSA Appendix A requires two five-minute overtime halves, followed by a shootout if still tied. Default extra reservation is 20 minutes; increase it for a larger shootout allowance. Match goals include overtime; enter shootout kicks separately on the GameCard. A tied card without a shootout winner cannot advance the tournament.

Preview and publish each round. Dates, allowed weekdays, field availability, coach/referee rest and daily limits use that division's Admin Schedule configuration. Later rounds start on a date after preceding-round fixtures. Missing capacity prevents publishing; unassigned referees are clearly identified and must be assigned and background-cleared before calling the game. Admin Schedule lists both phases and supports postponements, cancellations and replacement suggestions. Cancelled tournament games must be rescheduled and completed before advancing.

Coach **My Team** shows regular-season and tournament fixtures, final results, seed, loss count, byes and champion status. Future opponents are published only after their qualifying results are known.

Referee **GameCard** has Start game and End game controls. They record actual timestamps independently of scheduled kickoff, using club time for display. Managed cards must be ended before score confirmation/finalization. Starting a 0-0 game protects it from automated rescheduling. Signatures and finalization remain separate from ending play.

Admin **U6 Teams** selects team count and head coaches, imports the roster, records coach-parent links and directly assigns other athletes. U6 has no evaluation or draft. Head/assistant coach children automatically follow their parent's assigned team, including changes to assistants in My Team. Conflicting or unmatched parent links remain unassigned and require Admin review. Coaches' background checks remain managed in Admin Coaches.

Admin **Fields** can close a physical field indefinitely and record Damaged, Unplayable, Techform use or Other as its reason. Dated full-day/partial-day closures support temporary use. Save availability, then preview field schedule repair. It operates across divisions sharing that field. Optionally rework other upcoming unplayed fixtures to free capacity. Apply only after reviewing the proposed dates; if capacity is insufficient, no partial repair is applied. Recorded play and finalized matches stay fixed. Referee assignments are rechecked for replacement times, and previous bookings remain in match history.

These settings and fixtures use the existing workspace storage. Local test tabs share updates through the development workspace service; this change does not deploy a production scheduling backend.
